About Us

Your Partner in Intimate Apparel Manufacturing

Charlizi Factory was founded in 2018 with a mission to provide global brands with high-quality, ethically produced intimate apparel at competitive prices.

Factory Overview

Located in Puning, Guangdong - the heart of China's apparel manufacturing industry - our 5,000 ㎡ facility houses 200+ skilled workers producing over 5,000,000+ pcs garments annually.

We specialize in bras, bra sets, panties, plus size lingerie, silicone bras, shapewear, and custom sleepwear development. Our clients range from emerging DTC brands to established retailers across North America, Europe, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia.
